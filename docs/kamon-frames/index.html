木瓜 frame study

The four-lobed frame chosen in docs/direction-kamon-frame.md, with and without the inner line of the classic crest, and with the outer ring of 丸に木瓜 for reference. The page follows the system's light or dark setting. Built by tools/explore_frames.py.

With a thin inner line (chosen)

A band, a gap and a thin second line that follows the lobes: the double outline of the classic crest, kept slim so the motif loses little room. The line is under a pixel at 32px, so it softens there.

Without the inner line

One band. The motif gets the most room, and the lobes alone carry the crest.

With a split band

Two bands nearly as heavy as each other with a thin opening between them. Still a double outline, but it reads as one strong frame, and it holds a little better at 32px.

With an outer ring (丸に木瓜)

The other way to add a line: a plain circle around the whole crest. A common real variant, but the circle is what the icon looks like at a glance, which the direction ruled out, and everything inside gets smaller.

At 16px

Every style uses the same small mark: one heavier band and the motif without its cut-outs. An inner line or an outer ring would be under a pixel here.
