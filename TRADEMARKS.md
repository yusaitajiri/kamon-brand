# Names and logos

The style in this repository is open: the tokens, the rules, the tools and the platform kits (`ui.css`, the React components and the brand-ui skill) are MIT-licensed, and you're welcome to build on them. What stays reserved is what identifies Yusai Tajiri's apps:

- the names of the apps, such as Chidori;
- the logos: the 木瓜 frame with a motif inside, in `logo/` and `icons/` and wherever they appear.

`LICENSE` has the exact terms.

## You're welcome to

- Use the names and logos to refer to Yusai Tajiri's apps, for example in an article, a review, a link or a comparison.
- Use screenshots of the apps when you write about them.
- Build your own app with the tokens, the rules or a platform kit, under your own name and your own logo.
- Fork Yusai Tajiri's open-source apps and change them, as their own licenses allow.

## Please don't

- Release a fork or another product under the name or logo of one of Yusai Tajiri's apps. Give it your own name and icon.
- Use the names or logos in a way that suggests Yusai Tajiri made or endorsed something.
- Change, recolor or redraw the logos, or use the 木瓜 frame as the frame for your own family of icons.

## If you fork an app

Replace the app's name and its icon set, and point the app's accent and logo at your own. Everything else can stay. If you're unsure whether something is fine, open an issue and ask.
